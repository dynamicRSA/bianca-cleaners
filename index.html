Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers about our cleaning process, equipment, and scheduling.

How long do carpets and upholstery take to dry? ▾

Drying times depend on weather conditions, ventilation, ambient humidity, and fabric thickness. On warm, sunny Pretoria days with good airflow, carpets and couches typically dry within a matter of hours. In colder or rainy weather, drying will take longer. We utilize high-power industrial extraction machines that draw out the vast majority of water during the wash cycle to keep drying time as short as realistically possible.

Do you bring your own equipment and cleaning supplies? ▾

Yes, completely. Our mobile team arrives fully equipped with industrial-grade extraction machines, specialized cleaning detergents, extension leads, ladders, and tools. All we need from your premises is access to a standard electrical outlet and tap water.

Are your cleaning detergents safe for pets and children? ▾

Absolutely. We carefully select professional cleaning solutions that are non-toxic, eco-friendly, and biodegradable. Once clean and dry, all carpets, couches, and surfaces are 100% safe for dogs, cats, toddlers, and family members with allergies.

Can I trust your cleaning team in my home or office? ▾

Trust and honesty have been the backbone of Bianca Cleaners for over 36 years. We do not use casual day-laborers; our cleaners are permanent, background-vetted staff members. In addition, our field operations are supervised on-site by Pankosi to guarantee utmost care, respect for your property, and quality control.

How do I get an estimate and how does payment work? ▾

Quotes are 100% free with no obligation. The fastest method is to send photos or details of what you need cleaned via WhatsApp to (083) 265-6019, or give Rene and Shirley a call at the office. We accept convenient electronic bank transfers (EFT) and cash once the work is completed to your satisfaction.

Do you clean delicate Persian and heirloom rugs? ▾

Yes. We have decades of experience treating delicate natural fibers, wool, silk blends, and authentic Persian rugs using customized low-moisture techniques and pH-neutral solutions to protect dye integrity and fiber softness.
